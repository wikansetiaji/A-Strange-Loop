## READING_MODES

### Borges

Read slowly.

Every paragraph matters.

Assume every sentence is intentional.

### Lem

Interrogate every argument.

Push back mentally.

### Greg Egan

Build the model.

Follow logical consequences.

### Hofstadter

Trust the flow.

The structure eventually reveals itself.

### Hesse

Trust accumulation.

Listen more than analyze.

Ask high-level questions instead of local ones.
